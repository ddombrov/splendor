# Bookkeeping is animated, from a stored move log, with a speed setting

Because players make every choice and bookkeeping is automatic, the automatic steps are animated so people can follow them: paid tokens returning to the bag, the board refilling in spiral order, a new card sliding into the pyramid, a privilege moving to the opponent, and a royal card being claimed. Each animation lasts roughly a third to half a second and they play in order. Settings offers Normal, Fast and Off, with Normal as the default.

The opponent's move is replayed from a move log stored with the game, not worked out by comparing boards. A player who rejoins after missing several moves sees the board jump to the current state instead of replaying everything.
