# Players make every choice, and only bookkeeping is automatic

The game follows the physical game: nothing that involves a choice is done for the player. They pick which tokens to pay, take the gold before reserving, press Replenish themselves even when it is forced, and switch on each expansion discount themselves. Pure bookkeeping stays automatic, because an online opponent cannot do it for them. Bookkeeping means returning paid tokens to the bag, replacing pyramid cards, giving the penalty privilege, filling the board, and checking victory. These automatic steps are animated so players can follow them.

This deliberately deviates from the usual digital convention of auto-optimising payments.
